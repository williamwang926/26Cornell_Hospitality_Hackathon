Guest complaints ranked by net profit

Complaints are ranked by the annual net profit of their best solution (expected owner profit a year − annual cost), using this hotel's own model. ROI = net profit ÷ annual cost.

How expected profit is computed
  1. Stay Score gain of a solution = this hotel's own regression (Model B) gain for the complaint's topic — the prevent gain for Prevent solutions, the resolve gain for Resolve solutions — × the assumed solution effect × the share of guests in the target audience, converted at 30.2 Stay Score points per rating point.
  2. Annual cost = unit cost × annual uses.
  3. Expected profit (owner $/yr) = the Stay Score gain translated through the hotel-class price elasticity into rooms revenue, then rate flow-through + profit on extra room-nights + ancillary margin. The small low–high range beneath each figure brackets the assumptions.
  4. Net profit ($/yr) = expected profit − annual cost. Complaints are ranked by their best solution's net profit, and each complaint's solutions are ranked the same way (highest first). ROI = net profit ÷ annual cost.

  6. Guest rating lift = the 1–5 rating change expected for one guest whose complaint is fixed: this hotel's Model B weight for the case type (resolution weight for Resolve, prevention weight for Prevent; 0 if not positive) × the solution effect.
  7. "Fixing it entirely" = the Stay Score the hotel would gain if this complaint disappeared completely (Model B gain for the topic at 100% effect, all guests).
  8. Each solution is listed once, for the one audience it targets (family solutions are for Families only; general solutions are for All guests). Solutions for one complaint overlap, so they are alternatives, not additive.
  9. Urgent complaints are pinned at the very top, then Safety & security complaints ("Safety · escalate"): both go to Security / GM regardless of cost or rank.
  10. Several guest complaints can map to the same case type (model topic). They share one set of numbers, because the model scores the case type, not the wording.
Disclosures:
  • Historical rating association; not causal
  • Default cost/effect values are editable assumptions
  • Candidate coverage is not full classification accuracy
  • Safety/legal items override cost ranking
